Giraffe Event Spaces Ltd

St Paul's Church Hall Christmas Market — Trader Terms & Conditions

1. Definitions & Overview

  • Organiser: Giraffe Event Spaces Ltd (Director & Appointed First Aider: Kevin Wilson)
  • Trader: Any individual, business, or entity allocated trading space at the Event.
  • Event: St Paul's Church Hall Christmas Market, taking place at St Paul's Church Hall, Ashby, Scunthorpe DN16 2JR.
  • Pitch: The designated physical space allocated to the Trader by the Organiser.

2. Booking, Payment & Pitch Allocation

  • Confirmation: A booking is only confirmed once the online registration form is submitted, full payment (or required deposit) is cleared, and all requested compliance documentation is uploaded and verified.
  • Pitch Allocation: Pitch locations are assigned solely at the Organiser's discretion. The Organiser reserves the right to alter the layout or reallocate pitch spaces if necessary for operational, safety, or logistical reasons.
  • Non-Transferability: Pitch bookings are non-transferable. Traders may not sub-let, share, or transfer their space to a third party without prior written consent from the Organiser.

3. Compliance, Licensing & Document Submission

All required documentation—including your Risk Assessment—must be uploaded directly via the registration form on our website during the application process, or submitted at least 14 days prior to the Event date via email to hello@giraffeeventspaces.co.uk. Failure to provide valid documents may result in cancellation without a refund.

  • Risk Assessment & Method Statement (RAMS): Traders must complete and upload a written Risk Assessment covering set-up, operational hours, and pack-down directly through the online registration form on our website, or email it to hello@giraffeeventspaces.co.uk at least 14 days prior to the event.
  • Public Liability Insurance (PLI): Every Trader must upload proof of a valid PLI policy with a minimum cover of £1,000,000 (or local currency equivalent) valid for the event dates.
  • Food & Beverage Vendors (if applicable): Must provide proof of registration with a local authority/food safety governing body, Food Hygiene Rating (minimum score of 3 required), food handler hygiene certificates for all attending staff, and a complete Allergen Matrix displayed clearly at the pitch.
  • Waste Bins: General waste and recycling bins will be available across the event site for public and trader convenience; however, vendors remain responsible for maintaining cleanliness in their immediate pitch area.
  • Electrical Equipment (PAT): All electrical appliances brought onto site must carry a valid Portable Appliance Testing (PAT) certificate (tested within the last 12 months).
  • Gas & Fire Safety: Any gas appliances must have a current Gas Safe certificate (LPG/CP42 certification). Fire extinguishers/blankets appropriate to the setup must be provided by the Trader.

4. Operational Guidelines, Set-Up & Break-Down

  • Day-Before Set-Up (5th December): 05:00 PM – 07:00 PM. If you intend to set up the day before, you must contact the Organiser (Kevin Wilson, Director of Giraffe Event Spaces Ltd) in advance of the event via hello@giraffeeventspaces.co.uk.
  • Event-Day Timings: Set-up window: 08:00 AM – 09:30 AM (All stalls must be fully set up by 09:30 AM) | Trading hours: 10:00 AM – 04:00 PM | Pack-down time: Starts at 04:00 PM (Early pack-down prior to 04:00 PM is strictly prohibited for safety reasons).
  • Church Service & Drive Access: The church drive must be completely clear of all trader vehicles and obstacles by 09:30 AM prompt to allow access for the church service, with the exception of designated outdoor stall areas that have been allocated as approved trading pitches. Unloading must be completed and vehicles moved to the designated trader parking area before 09:30 AM.
  • Vehicle Movement: Vehicle movement on site during public event hours (10:00 AM – 04:00 PM) is strictly prohibited. Vehicles may only re-enter or move on site for pack-down after 04:00 PM when permitted by event marshals, or as otherwise agreed with the Organiser in exceptional circumstances such as sudden illness or emergencies.
  • On-the-Day Emergencies & First Aid: Kevin Wilson is First Aid at Work qualified and will act as the appointed First Aider on the day of the event. In the event of a medical emergency or operational issue, please call Kevin directly on 07466125584.
  • Trading Restrictions: Traders may only sell items specified and approved in their original application. The sale of illegal, counterfeit, age-restricted (without proper licensing), or offensive goods is strictly forbidden.
  • Waste & Environment: Traders are responsible for keeping their pitch clean and tidy during trading hours. All commercial waste, wastewater, greywater, packaging, and single-use plastic items must be removed from the site by the Trader at the end of the Event. Leaving waste behind will incur a cleanup fee of £100, as the Organiser will need to hire a licensed waste removal van to collect and legally dispose of any rubbish left on site.

5. Power, Equipment & Facilities

  • Power Supply: Power supply is available free of charge as part of your pitch booking fee, but must be requested in advance via the online registration form. Traders requesting power must supply their own exterior-grade extension leads (minimum 25m).
  • Generators: Generators are permitted only for outdoor pitches. All generators must be silent/low-noise models, well-maintained, safely positioned away from public thoroughfares, and listed in your Risk Assessment. Generators are strictly prohibited indoors.
  • Structures: All gazebos, marquees, and temporary structures must be commercial grade and heavily weighted (minimum 15kg–20kg per leg) or securely pegged.

6. Cancellations, Refunds & Force Majeure

  • Cancellation by Trader: Cancellations made within 7 calendar days of paying the booking fee are eligible for a refund. Cancellations made after 7 calendar days from the booking fee payment date are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances, as we will not have enough time to find a replacement trader, resulting in a financial loss for the company.
  • Cancellation by Organiser / Force Majeure: The Organiser reserves the right to cancel or curtail the Event due to severe weather, safety threats, government restrictions, or other unforeseen events beyond reasonable control (Force Majeure). In the event of full cancellation prior to site opening, traders will be rebooked onto the next available event at a date that is convenient for the trader. The Organiser is not liable for any consequential loss, travel costs, or loss of revenue incurred by the Trader.

7. Limitation of Liability & Indemnity

  • Loss or Damage: The Organiser accepts no liability or responsibility for any loss, damage, theft, or destruction of Trader stock, equipment, or personal property during the Event, set-up, or pack-down.
  • Indemnity: The Trader agrees to indemnify the Organiser against all claims, liabilities, damages, costs, and expenses arising from any breach of these Terms, negligence, or misconduct by the Trader or their staff.

8. Trader Conduct, Venue Respect & Termination

  • Traders and their staff must conduct themselves professionally, politely, and respectfully toward event attendees, venue staff, church congregation members, and neighboring traders.
  • Noise levels must be kept respectful, especially during church service hours.
  • The Organiser reserves the right to immediately terminate the trading agreement and request the removal of any Trader who breaches safety regulations, fails to adhere to these terms, or engages in disruptive or aggressive behavior. No refunds will be issued in such cases.
