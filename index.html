Giraffe Event Spaces Ltd

St Paul's Church Hall Christmas Market — Trader Terms & Conditions

1. Definitions & Overview

  • Organiser: Giraffe Event Spaces Ltd (Director & Appointed First Aider: Kevin Wilson)
  • Trader: Any individual, business, or entity allocated trading space at the Event.
  • Event: St Paul's Church Hall Christmas Market, taking place at St Paul's Church Hall, Ashby, Scunthorpe DN16 2JR.
  • Pitch: The designated physical space allocated to the Trader by the Organiser.

2. Booking, Payment & Pitch Allocation

  • Confirmation: A booking is only confirmed once the online registration form is submitted, full payment (or required deposit) is cleared, and all requested compliance documentation is uploaded and verified.
  • Pitch Allocation: Pitch locations are assigned solely at the Organiser's discretion. The Organiser reserves the right to alter the layout or reallocate pitch spaces if necessary for operational, safety, or logistical reasons.
  • Non-Transferability: Pitch bookings are non-transferable. Traders may not sub-let, share, or transfer their space to a third party without prior written consent from the Organiser.

3. Compliance, Licensing & Document Submission

All required documentation—including your Risk Assessment—must be uploaded directly via the registration form on our website during the application process, or submitted at least 14 days prior to the Event date via email to hello@giraffeeventspaces.co.uk. Failure to provide valid documents may result in cancellation without a refund.

  • RAMS & PLI: Traders must complete and upload a written Risk Assessment covering set-up, operational hours, and pack-down, or email it to hello@giraffeeventspaces.co.uk at least 14 days prior. Every trader must hold valid PLI with minimum cover of £1,000,000.
  • Food Vendors: Minimum Food Hygiene Rating of 3 required, hygiene certificates for all staff, and an Allergen Matrix displayed. General waste and recycling bins will be available across the event site.
  • PAT & Gas Safety: Electrical appliances require valid PAT testing (within 12 months). Gas appliances require a valid Gas Safe certificate.

4. Operational Guidelines, Set-Up & Break-Down

  • Day-Before Set-Up (5th December): 05:00 PM – 07:00 PM. (If setting up day before, contact Kevin Wilson in advance via hello@giraffeeventspaces.co.uk).
  • Event Timings: Set-up window: 08:00 AM – 09:30 AM (fully set up by 09:30 AM) | Trading hours: 10:00 AM – 04:00 PM | Pack-down starts: 04:00 PM.
  • Church Service Drive Access: Drive must be completely clear of all trader vehicles/obstacles by 09:30 AM prompt for church service, with exception of designated outdoor stall pitches.
  • Vehicle Movement: Strictly prohibited on site between 10:00 AM – 04:00 PM except for agreed emergencies or illness.
  • On-the-Day Emergencies & First Aid: Kevin Wilson is First Aid at Work qualified. Call Kevin directly on 07466125584.
  • Waste & Single-Use Plastics: All commercial waste and single-use plastics must be removed at pack-down. Leaving waste behind incurs a £100 fee for removal van hire.

5. Power, Facilities & Cancellations

  • Power Supply: Free of charge when requested in advance. Traders must supply 25m exterior extension leads. Silent/low-noise generators allowed for outdoor pitches only.
  • Cancellation Policy: Refunds available within 7 calendar days of paying booking fee. Cancellations after 7 days are strictly non-refundable due to loss incurred from inability to re-book pitch space.
  • Force Majeure: If cancelled by Organiser, traders will be rebooked onto the next available event at a date convenient for the trader.
