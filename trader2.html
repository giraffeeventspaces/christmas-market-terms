GIRAFFE EVENT SPACES LTD

The Church Of The Resurrection Christmas Market — Trader Terms & Conditions

1. DEFINITIONS & OVERVIEW

  • Organiser: Giraffe Event Spaces Ltd (Director & Appointed First Aider: Kevin Wilson).
  • Trader: Any individual, business, or entity allocated trading space at the Event.
  • Event: The Church Of The Resurrection Christmas Market, taking place at Mirfield Road, Scunthorpe DN15 8AN.
  • Pitch: The designated physical space allocated to the Trader by the Organiser.

2. BOOKING, PAYMENT & PITCH ALLOCATION

  • Confirmation: A booking is only confirmed once full payment is received along with all required compliance documentation (insurance, risk assessments, hygiene rating if applicable).
  • Pitch Allocation: Pitch locations are assigned solely at the Organiser's discretion. The Organiser reserves the right to adjust pitch layouts or rearrange locations for operational, health, safety, or logistical reasons.
  • Non-Transferability: Pitch bookings are non-transferable. Traders may not sub-let, share, or transfer their space to another party without prior written consent from the Organiser.

3. COMPLIANCE, LICENSING & DOCUMENT SUBMISSION

Mandatory Documentation: All traders must submit relevant documentation (Public Liability Insurance, Risk Assessments, etc.) upon applying. Failure to do so will result in cancellation without refund.

  • Risk Assessment & Method Statement (RAMS): Every trader must submit a written Risk Assessment covering set-up, operation, and dismantle of their stall. A template can be provided if required, but must be returned at least 14 days prior to the event.
  • Public Liability Insurance (PLI): Every trader must hold valid PLI covering with a minimum cover of £5,000,000 for the event duration. A copy must be uploaded upon booking.
  • Food & Beverage Vendors (if applicable): Food business registration certificate from your local council, valid Food Hygiene Certificate for all staff (Level 2 minimum), Food Hygiene Rating (minimum 4 stars preferred), and an Allergen Matrix displayed clearly at the pitch.
  • Silenced Power: Outdoor vendors using generators must supply silent noise-attenuated generators only. Standard noisy open-frame generators are strictly forbidden on site.
  • Electrical Equipment (PAT): All electrical equipment brought on-site must be validly PAT tested, displaying valid PAT labels with test date matching.
  • Gas & Fire Safety: Gas equipment must have a valid CP42 certificate dated within the last 12 months. Suitable fire extinguishers (Co2/Foam) and fire blankets must be provided by the trader.

4. OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES, SET-UP & BREAK-DOWN

  • Day Before Set-Up (12th December): 17:00 PM – 19:00 PM. Early access for designated indoor / outdoor setups. Pitch locations will be coordinated in advance. Please notify us if required via hello@giraffeeventspaces.co.uk.
  • Event Day Timings: Setup opens at 08:00 AM - 09:30 AM (All stalls must be operational by 09:45 AM). Trading Hours: 10:00 AM - 16:00 PM. Breakdown: 16:00 PM onwards (No early packing down permitted prior to 16:00 PM under any circumstances).
  • Church Service & Unload Access: The main driveway must be completely clear of all vehicle traffic and obstructions by 09:15 AM sharp to allow access for the church service and the arrival of congregation members and visitors. No late vehicle movement or unloading inside the service window will be permitted, and strict enforcement applies.
  • Vehicle Movement: Vehicle access for setup is strictly permitted between 08:00 AM - 09:15 AM only. All vehicles must be unloaded, moved, and parked in off-site spaces before 09:15 AM sharp. On-site vehicle movements during live market hours are strictly forbidden without permission from event management.
  • On-Site Contingencies & Support: Kevin Wilson is designated event coordinator and first aider on-site. On the day of the event, for urgent queries or assistance, please call Kevin directly on 07469233304.
  • Trading Restrictions: Traders may only sell items listed and approved on their application form. Sale of illegal, counterfeit, hazardous, or prohibited items is strictly forbidden.
  • Waste & Cleanliness: Traders are responsible for keeping their pitch clean and clear of rubbish throughout the day. All rubbish, packaging, and waste generated by the stall must be taken away by the Trader at the end of the event. Designated spillages/waste left behind will incur a clean-up fee and may affect future application decisions.

5. POWER, EQUIPMENT & FACILITIES

  • Power Supply: Power supply is limited and must be booked and paid for in advance. No unapproved heavy appliances may be plugged in. Access to power cables must be protected using rubber cable ramps (traders must supply).
  • Generators: No traders are permitted to bring generators. All generators must be silent or low-noise generators and must be pre-approved. Fuel storage must be strictly managed as outlined in your Risk Assessment. Suitable fire protection required.
  • Gazebos: All gazebos, canopies, and temporary structures must be commercial grade and weighted down with a minimum 15kg weight per leg on every corner.

6. CANCELLATIONS, REFUNDS & FORCE MAJEURE

  • Cancellation by Trader: Cancellations made more than 14 days prior to event will receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made within 14 days of the event date are non-refundable. All booking fees/deposits are non-transferable to other dates/events. Requests for cancellation must be made in writing to the Organiser.
  • Cancellation by Organiser / Force Majeure: The Organiser reserves the right to cancel or postpone the event in cases of extreme adverse weather, severe safety warnings, government restrictions, or other unexpected events outside of our reasonable control. In such cases, pitch fees may be rolled over to a rescheduled date. The Organiser accepts no liability for secondary losses, loss of stock, or loss of earnings incurred by the Trader.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & INDEMNITY

  • Loss or Damage: The Organiser accepts no liability or responsibility for any loss, damage, theft, or destruction of Trader equipment, products, or personal property during the Event, set-up, or breakdown.
  • Indemnity: The Trader agrees to fully indemnify the Organiser against all claims, liabilities, damages, costs, and expenses arising from any breach of these Terms, negligence, or wrongdoing by the Trader or their staff.

8. TRADER CONDUCT, VENUE RESPECT & TERMINATION

  • Traders must maintain professional, respectful, courteous behavior toward staff, visitors, and fellow traders at all times. Unacceptable behavior, offensive language, or aggression will result in immediate removal.
  • Traders must respect the venue grounds, property, and surrounding residential area.
  • The Organiser reserves the right to immediately terminate the trading agreement and request immediate removal of the Trader and their stall for non-compliance with these Terms or safety rules. No refund will be issued in such circumstances.
