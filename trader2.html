The Church Of The Resurrection Christmas Market — Trader Terms & Conditions

1. Definitions & Overview

  • Organiser: Giraffe Event Spaces Ltd (Director & Appointed First Aider: Kevin Wilson).
  • Trader: Any individual, business, or entity allocated trading space at the Event.
  • Event: The Church Of The Resurrection Christmas Market, taking place at Mirfield Road, Scunthorpe DN15 8AN.
  • Pitch: The designated physical space allocated to the Trader by the Organiser.

2. Event Timings & Access Framework

  • Market Hours: The Event will run live on Sunday 13th December between 10:00am and 4:00pm.
  • Standard Setup Window: On-site access for regular event-day setup begins at 8:00am on Sunday 13th December. All vendors must be fully built and operational by 9:45am.
  • Early Setup Window: Early setup access will be available for designated vendors on the day prior to the event (Saturday 12th December) between 5:00pm and 7:00pm. Pitch allocations for this window will be coordinated in advance.

3. Booking, Payment & Pitch Allocation

  • Confirmation: A booking is only confirmed once the registration parameters are verified, full payment is cleared, and all compliance documentation is filed.
  • Pitch Allocation: Pitch locations are assigned solely at the Organiser's discretion. The Organiser reserves the right to alter layout boundaries for operational safety.
  • Non-Transferability: Pitch bookings are non-transferable. Traders may not sub-let or share their space without prior written consent from the Organiser.

4. Compliance, Liability & Safety

  • Public Liability Insurance: All vendors must hold active Public Liability Insurance up to £5M, which must remain valid throughout the entire duration of the market workspace footprint.
  • Cancellations: Cancellations processed within 14 days of the targeted launch layout space remain strictly non-refundable due to pre-allocated operational expenditures.

5. Stall Requirements & Equipment

  • Tables & Chairs: Traders must supply their own tables, chairs, and display structures unless explicitly booked in advance through the Organiser.
  • Electrical Safety: All electrical equipment used on site must be PAT tested and carry a valid inspection sticker. The use of unauthorized heating appliances is strictly prohibited.
  • Gazebos & Weights: Outdoor gazebos must be securely weighted on all legs (minimum 15kg per leg). Leg straps and tie-downs must be inspected before trading begins.

6. Food & Health Regulations

  • Food Hygiene: Food traders must hold a minimum 4-star Food Hygiene Rating, register with their local authority, and provide copies of relevant certificates prior to the event.
  • Allergen Information: Clear allergen warnings must be visibly displayed on all food products in compliance with Food Standards Agency guidelines.
  • Waste Disposal: Traders are responsible for disposing of hot oil, waste water, and food refuse in designated waste containers only.

7. Waste Management & Site Cleanliness

  • Pitch Maintenance: Traders must keep their allocated pitch area clean, tidy, and free of trip hazards throughout the trading period.
  • Post-Event Cleanup: All rubbish, packaging, and unsold stock must be removed by the Trader at the conclusion of the market. Failure to leave the pitch clean may result in a cleanup fee.

8. Conduct & General Regulations

  • Professional Behavior: Traders and their staff must maintain a professional and polite demeanor toward visitors, fellow traders, and event stewards at all times.
  • Noise & Music: Amplified music or sound equipment is not permitted without explicit written approval from the Organiser.
  • Organiser Instructions: Traders must follow all instructions given by event stewards and emergency services personnel promptly.
